Sovereign Edge-Link (SEL)

AI that keeps working when the internet is gone

Architecture

In plain English

SEL is a research design for artificial intelligence that does not depend on the cloud. Instead of one big model doing everything, it uses a small team of specialist roles — one plans, one acts, one checks safety, one pulls the answer together — so decisions stay controlled even in places with weak or no signal. Built for tough, offline environments. Still research-stage; human control stays central.

Ghostprint

Check if your iPhone has been hacked or tracked

Security

In plain English

Ghostprint is a private tool that looks for signs someone may be secretly watching your iPhone — spyware, odd remote-control settings, or suspicious apps. You plug the phone into your own computer over USB. Nothing is sent to the cloud. You get a clear report in everyday language. Built for ordinary people and high-risk users alike.

ESFT

A different idea about how the universe expands

Research

In plain English

ESFT (Emergent Spacetime Flux Theory) is independent physics research. It explores whether cosmic expansion and structure can be explained with a “flux” model of spacetime, instead of leaning only on unseen dark components. Early-stage work, tested against public datasets. Claims stay provisional until stronger validation.
