Fork · Plain language

MyPaiOS

A local-first AI workspace that runs on hardware you control — chat, agents, research, video analysis, email triage and more.

Attribution:
This is a fork of mypaios/mypaios by Vishal Pawar. That project itself builds on the earlier Odysseus codebase (Odysseus Contributors / nubs). Full credit belongs to the original authors. Vectis Ops maintains this fork for local evaluation and experimentation.

What it is, in plain English

MyPaiOS aims to give you the experience of a modern AI assistant (chat, multi-step research, coding help, document handling, optional agents) while keeping the core work on your own machine. You can point it at local models (via Ollama and similar) or optional external endpoints.

Local-first design

Core functionality is designed to run without mandatory cloud accounts for everyday use.

Multiple capabilities

Chat, research pipelines, agent patterns, video analysis, email triage and more are present in the upstream project.

Hardware-aware

Includes guidance and tooling oriented around what models your machine can realistically run.

Honest limits

  • This is a maintained fork for study and local use — not a claim of original authorship.
  • Setup complexity and model quality depend on your hardware and the upstream project state.
  • Always check the original repository for the latest licences, acknowledgements and security notes.
