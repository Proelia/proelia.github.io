Vespera

A private AI assistant that runs on your own computer — not a company cloud chat box. Free, open source, and designed so ordinary people can install it.

The simple idea: You download language models onto a laptop or desktop you control. Vespera is the software layer around that: chat, different “personalities,” optional extras (images, voice, search), and a clear preference for staying offline when you want.

What you can do with it

Chat privately

Talk to a capable AI on your machine. Your conversations do not have to leave your network.

Switch personalities

Use different written “personas” — calm analyst, planner, and more — as simple text files you can edit.

Use a simple control panel or a terminal

Prefer a browser window or a pure text interface. Both are supported.

Optional extras

Image tools, voice in/out, web search, and basic network checks — only if you install those backends. If they are offline, the rest still works.

Works on common computers

Linux, Mac, and Windows. An Android remote client (phone UI talking to your home computer on the local network) is in active testing.

Who it is for

  • People who want AI without mandatory cloud accounts
  • Anyone experimenting with local models at home or in a lab
  • Users who care about offline operation after models are downloaded

Honest limits

  • You need a decent computer and disk space for models
  • Setup is easier if you are comfortable following install steps (or have someone technical help once)
  • Quality depends on which local models you choose
  • Optional tools need extra programs; they are not magic built-ins
Status: Actively developed. Main path is the multi-platform Python stack. Open source under a permissive licence.

Where to get it

Source and docs: github.com/vectisops/Vespera
