Orientation

MyPaiOS is a substantial local-first AI workspace. It combines chat, agent loops, research pipelines, optional media/email tooling and hardware-aware model guidance. The majority of the codebase originates from the upstream authors; this fork exists for local evaluation under Vectis Ops.

cd mypaios
