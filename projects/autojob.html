AutoJob

A personal job-search assistant built for Australia — especially Brisbane and South East Queensland. Broader coverage, ranked for you, maximum 30 matches, and local de-duplication so later searches stay fresh.

The simple idea: Pull a larger but still manageable set of listings, score them against your preferences and résumé, then show only the strongest matches. Everything stays on your machine. No cloud ranking, no bulk harvesting.

What you can do with it

Broader search, tighter results

Looks further than a normal job-board first page so niche or poorly titled roles are less likely to be missed, then returns a clean top-30 list.

Ranked for you

Set locations, role types, include/exclude keywords, salary range, and optionally upload a résumé. Scoring reflects your background, not generic keywords alone.

Fresh results over time

Private local history filters out jobs you’ve already seen so later runs surface new opportunities.

One-click apply

Top matches appear as cards with an Apply Now button that simply opens the official job page. AutoJob never submits applications for you.

Light company context

Quick background on employers, with notes that are useful when considering roles in South East Queensland.

Who it is for

  • One person hunting for their next role in Australia (especially SEQ / Brisbane)
  • Anyone who wants wider coverage without drowning in noise
  • Users who prefer private, local tools over cloud job platforms

Honest limits

  • Requires free API credentials for some sources (e.g. Adzuna) and optional one-time browser login for others
  • Quality of ranking depends on the preferences and résumé you supply
  • Designed for a single user — not a recruitment or agency tool
Status: Actively usable personal tool. Open source. Private by design — résumé, history, and sessions stay on your computer.
