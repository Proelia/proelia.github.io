Sovereign Edge-Link (SEL)

AI designed to keep working when the internet is gone — a small team of specialist roles that plan, act, check safety, and agree before risky moves. Research-stage. Human control stays first-class.

In one sentence: SEL is a blueprint for offline multi-agent AI: four specialised minds share a fast local workspace, argue, and only then act — so one runaway model cannot silently do something dangerous.

Why it exists

Cloud AI fails when signal is jammed, denied, or simply absent. SEL targets those environments: edge devices, field systems, and contested networks where latency and sovereignty matter. The design separates reaction, planning, inhibition, and integration so safety gates are structural, not an afterthought.

The four roles (plain + technical)

Executor

Fast reactions from sensors (GPS, motion, proximity). Proposes immediate moves. Analogous to instinctive response — high frequency, short horizon.

Strategist

Reads mission intent and longer plans. Pattern recognition, prioritisation, deception-aware planning. Higher-level reasoning over the brief.

Critic

Safety and rules gate. Enforces constraints (legal, thermal, power, ROE). Can inhibit or kill a proposal before hardware executes it.

Synthesizer

Merges the debate into a single coherent output: machine-facing commands and human-readable status. Integration layer.

Shared Thought Space (STS)

Agents do not only talk over slow network sockets. SEL assumes a high-speed shared workspace (conceptually a RAM-backed blackboard) where each role can observe intent in near real time. That enables hardware-level interrupts: the Critic can flip a kill-bit before a prohibited command reaches actuators. Practical loops today are exercised through related lab tooling (see Vespera); full STS hardware binding remains research maturity.

Application domains (research framing)

  • Denied / contested environments — where cloud-dependent stacks fail
  • Resilience & border / infrastructure sensing — offline inference hubs
  • Industrial & remote ops — battery-aware, weather-aware edge agents
  • Digital environments — same logic layer as a defensive coordination agent
Honesty on maturity: Architecture and role design are the mature artefacts. End-to-end hardware productisation, certified ROE stacks, and production fleet deployment are not claimed as shipped product.
