Ghostprint

Check whether your iPhone shows signs of being hacked or secretly tracked. Private. Offline. Built so non-technical people can understand the report.

The simple idea: Spyware sometimes leaves digital fingerprints. Ghostprint plugs your phone into your own computer, makes a careful copy of its data, and looks for those fingerprints — plus odd remote-control settings and suspicious apps. Nothing is uploaded to a company cloud.

What it does, step by step

1. Connects by USB cable

Same idea as plugging in for a normal computer backup. You unlock the phone and tap Trust.

2. Makes a careful copy

It examines that copy, not your live phone while you are using it.

3. Looks for known spyware traces

Uses a respected free engine (originally from Amnesty International’s work) that knows many commercial spyware fingerprints.

4. Checks settings and apps

Flags unknown “management” profiles and apps with suspicious names.

5. Writes a plain report

You get a readable summary and practical next steps.

Who it is for

  • Anyone worried their phone may have been compromised
  • Journalists, activists, or higher-risk users
  • People who refuse commercial “phone cleaner” apps that demand cloud accounts

What it can and cannot do

Can help find

  • Known commercial spyware families (including some related to high-end tools like Pegasus)
  • Unexpected remote-management profiles
  • Roughly suspicious app names

Cannot

  • Guarantee the phone is perfectly clean
  • Watch the phone in real time
  • Automatically “fix” infection
  • Work if the phone stays locked
If something looks serious: Save the report. Remove unknown profiles on the phone. Change your Apple ID password from another device. Consider a full erase and set-up as new — without restoring from a suspect backup. High-risk users should contact a digital security specialist.

Safety basics

  • Only check phones you own or have clear permission to examine
  • Prefer a clean computer you do not use for banking
  • Leave the phone charged — backups can take a long time
  • Keep iOS updated
